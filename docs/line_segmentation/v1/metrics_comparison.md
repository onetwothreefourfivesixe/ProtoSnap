# Metrics: eBL set versus CDLI Old Assyrian set

Generated by `python -m line_cropping.report.aggregate_metrics` on 2026-09-16 from the result tables under `ebl_tablets/` and `fat-cross_processed/`. The per-dataset tables with every value and its source file are `ebl_tablets/metrics/metrics.csv` and `fat-cross_processed/metrics/metrics.csv` (not tracked in git). The definitions are as follows. A line is matched if it lies within 0.35 pitch of a truth centre. Precision is computed only on faces with coverage >= 0.9. First-line accuracy is the share of faces whose first line lies within 0.5 pitch of the truth. Count match means that the number of predicted lines equals the transliteration count.

**Read the comparison with two caveats.** The two sets are scored against different truths. The eBL lines come from the eBL sign annotations on all faces, while the CDLI lines come from 88 hand-labelled faces whose starting positions were the profile fit itself. The crops are also cut differently: on eBL they are cut from detector-box gaps, and on CDLI they are cut from midpoints between predicted centres. Where a number is not comparable, it is marked.

## 3. Line finding by method

The eBL rows score all single-column faces against the eBL line truth. The CDLI rows score the 88 hand-labelled faces. `det_n` clusters the detector boxes and forces the count to the ATF. `det` clusters the detector boxes with a free count. `dp` is the projection-profile dynamic programme with the ATF count. `comb` is a rigid comb with the ATF count. `free` takes the profile peaks with no prior.

| method | set | faces | recall | precision | first line < 0.5 pitch | count match |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| det_n | eBL | 979 | 0.90 | 0.90 | 90% | 86% |
| det_n | CDLI | 82 | 0.43 | 0.37 | 33% | 49% |
| det | eBL | 979 | 0.86 | 0.90 | 87% | 0% |
| det | CDLI | 82 | 0.35 | 0.49 | 34% | 9% |
| dp | eBL | 980 | 0.62 | 0.59 | 30% | 86% |
| dp | CDLI | 88 | 0.89 | 0.76 | 69% | 49% |
| comb | eBL | 980 | 0.60 | 0.58 | 30% | 86% |
| comb | CDLI | 88 | 0.84 | 0.73 | 64% | 49% |
| free | eBL | 980 | 0.49 | 0.59 | 25% | 5% |

The chosen method is `det_n (detector boxes + ATF count)` for eBL and `dp (projection-profile dynamic programme + ATF count)` for CDLI. The ranking reverses between the sets. The detector finds 51 boxes per face on eBL photographs and 22 on the Old Assyrian ones, so its line grouping starves there. The profile method, which plateaus at 0.62 recall on eBL, reaches 0.89 on the labelled Old Assyrian faces.
